What We
Offer.

Everything happens where your vehicle is parked. Open a card to see what is included, then text us to book.

Complete Detail

Inside and out, start to finish.

One visit, the whole vehicle. We take the interior and exterior from lived-in to like-new so you enjoy the drive again.

Text To Book
  • Foam soak and careful hand wash
  • Wheels scrubbed, tires dressed
  • Vents, seams and console brushed out
  • Seats, carpets and mats cleaned
  • Glass and screens wiped streak-free
  • Microfiber hand dry and a final check
  • Engine bay available on request

Exterior Detailing

A thick soak, a gentle hand wash.

A blanket of foam loosens the grime, then a gentle hand wash and a towel dry bring the gloss back to your paint, wheels and tires included.

Text To Book
  • Foam pre-soak
  • Hand wash with a wash mitt
  • Wheels and wheel wells scrubbed by hand
  • Brake dust and road grime removed
  • Tires cleaned and dressed
  • Microfiber hand dry
  • Streak-free finish by hand

Interior Detailing

Cabin care, down to the seams.

Dust, crumbs and everyday wear come out of every corner. The inside of your vehicle gets vacuumed and cleaned by hand until it feels fresh again.

Text To Book
  • Full interior vacuuming
  • Vents, buttons and seams worked with brushes
  • Dash, console and door panels wiped down
  • Seats cleaned and refreshed
  • Carpets and mats scrubbed
  • Screens and glass cleaned

Clay Bar Treatment

Smooth paint, not just clean paint.

A clay bar lifts the bonded grit and road film a wash leaves behind, so the paint feels smooth before any wax or polish goes on.

Text To Book
  • Wash before the clay pass
  • Clay bar worked over the paint
  • Bonded grit and overspray lifted
  • Surface checked by hand for smoothness
  • Pairs well with a wax or polish

Car Waxing

Gloss and protection, applied by hand.

A hand-applied wax adds depth to the paint and a protective layer on top, then it is buffed off by hand for a clean, deep shine.

Text To Book
  • Hand wash and dry first
  • Wax applied by hand
  • Buffed off with clean microfiber
  • Trim and edges wiped clean

Polishing & Scratch Removal

Bring the clarity back.

Machine polishing works out dullness and light scratches so the paint looks deeper and cleaner. Text us about any scratches and we will tell you what is realistic.

Text To Book
  • Wash and prep before polishing
  • Machine polish over the paint
  • Light scratches and swirls worked out
  • Deeper scratches assessed first
  • Panels wiped down and checked

Boats, RVs & Big Rigs

Bigger surfaces, same standard.

Sun, salt and road film hit big surfaces hard. We bring the polish, the foam and the brushes to your dock, yard or driveway. Text us the size and we will take it from there.

Text To Book
  • Boat hulls washed and machine polished
  • Foam wash and hand scrub for RVs and trucks
  • Wheels, tires and wheel wells
  • Interiors cleaned
  • Done at your dock, yard or driveway
  • Priced by size and condition
